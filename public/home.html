Questions

Everything else you might ask.

What is DropQR?

A free file-sharing service built for instant transfers. Drop a file → get a link, a short code and a QR → whoever opens it receives the file. The passage is temporary; then the file simply ceases to exist.

Is it really unlimited?

No quotas, no accounts, no size games: send one photo or a multi-gigabyte project, as often as you want. The hard limit is whatever the host disk and your connection can carry — currently up to 10 Go per file.

How long does a link stay alive?

You choose at send time, from 5 minutes to 24 hours. It also dies instantly after the first download if you ticked that option — and you can cut it yourself anytime with your private key from the Transfers page.

How do I receive a file?

Open the shared link, scan the QR with your phone camera, or type the 7-character code on the receive page. Videos stream inline with a built-in player; everything else downloads directly.

Do I need an account?

No. DropQR is anonymous by design. The optional Discord sign-in only labels your uploads for the instance staff — sending and receiving never require it.
