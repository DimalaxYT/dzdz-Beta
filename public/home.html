What is DropQR?

DropQR is a free file-sharing service built for instant transfers. Drop a file, get a link and a QR code — whoever opens it receives the file. No account, no friction.

Is it really unlimited?

Yes. There are no quotas and no size caps: send a single photo or a multi-gigabyte video project, as many times as you want.

How long does a link stay alive?

By design, transfers are temporary: the file is removed after the first download or when its timer runs out — short-lived by default, extendable up to 24 h per transfer.

How do I receive a file?

Open the shared link, scan the QR code with any camera, or type the transfer code on the receive page. The download starts instantly.
